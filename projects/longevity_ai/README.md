## What it solves

LongevityAI combines public human biomarker data, published biological-age mathematics and machine learning to support responsible aging-research intelligence.

## Evidence

- **Human data:** NHANES biomarker data and population reference benchmarking.
- **Geroscience:** published PhenoAge implementation and cross-check.
- **Validation:** PhenoAge trajectory cross-check correlation **0.9977**.
- **Machine learning:** selected HistGradientBoosting model with locked-test **MAE 8.83 years**, **RMSE 11.22 years**, **R² 0.572**.
- **Explainability:** predictive biomarker importance and transparent one-at-a-time sensitivity analysis.
- **Reliability:** subgroup performance audit and cross-model robustness checks.
- **Decision intelligence:** a digital-twin what-if engine for multi-biomarker research scenarios.
- **Product thinking:** architecture for longitudinal biomarkers, multi-clock aging, uncertainty, monitoring and prospective intervention trials.

## Why it matters

The goal is not to claim that an algorithm can reverse aging. The system is designed to help researchers and longevity teams measure aging-related signals, benchmark cohorts, generate hypotheses and prioritize what should be tested prospectively.

## Scientific boundary

Model-estimated age and scenario sensitivity are **research signals**, not diagnoses, prescriptions or proof that changing a biomarker causes rejuvenation or extends lifespan.
